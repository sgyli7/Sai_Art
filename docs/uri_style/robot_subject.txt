SUBJECT BRIEF — controlled robot test
Design a new URI-style interpretation of the compact super-deformed heroic robot shown in the two SUBJECT reference images. Preserve the oversized helmet, short broad torso, stout limbs, broad white thigh/shin armor, large angular blue feet, blue torso and forearms, and its recognizably squat proportions. Preserve the gold/yellow swept V-shaped helmet horns, the central red faceted helmet jewel, narrow green eye strip, gray/silver slotted faceplate, paired red shoulder shields bearing gold stylized dragon silhouettes, the red curved claws at the upper chest, and the row of round yellow/gold waist fittings with a larger central circular fitting. These are identity features, not optional decoration. Do not borrow the head or silhouette of any robot on the STYLE board. Keep the robot's blue/ivory structure; translate bright gold to painted warm ochre and reserve red for the existing identity accents. Keep two arms, two legs, and coherent articulated hands/joints.

One full-body three-quarter view, standing in a confident broad stance with both hands relaxed, the complete feet and horns visible. Frame as a portrait concept illustration on a simple pale ivory ground with a large clear blue backdrop and a small cool cast shadow. Draw subtly layered serviceable joints and localized enamel wear while keeping the original armor shapes legible. A single robot only. No dragon creature, other people, buildings, weapon, floating panels, product-photo backdrop, titles, or watermark. This is an illustrated design reinterpretation, not a photograph of a toy.
